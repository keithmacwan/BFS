Equipment Infrastructure Readiness

Before the equipment arrives, make sure the facility is ready for it.

BFS helps laboratories, facilities teams and equipment partners determine whether a proposed site can support equipment that is being purchased, leased, rented, relocated or temporarily installed.

Why It Matters

Equipment specifications do not automatically mean the building is ready.

A successful installation depends on more than a purchase order. Power, cooling, exhaust, specialty utilities, structural conditions, access, monitoring and commissioning may all affect whether the equipment can operate as intended.

01Wrong or insufficient power

Voltage, phase, panel capacity, emergency coverage or restoration sequence may not match the equipment need.

02Hidden heat and HVAC load

New equipment can add heat, humidity or ventilation demand that the room or air system was not designed to absorb.

03Utilities or access discovered too late

Gases, water, drainage, exhaust, cooling, vibration or movement-path constraints can delay delivery or installation.

What BFS Reviews

The infrastructure behind the equipment.

The review is tailored to the equipment type, laboratory use, manufacturer requirements and project stage.

01

Normal & Emergency Power

Voltage, phase, connected load, panel capacity, receptacles, emergency-power coverage, ATS dependencies and restoration sequence.

02

HVAC & Heat Rejection

Equipment heat output, room cooling, temperature and humidity limits, ventilation requirements and failure conditions.

03

Exhaust & Containment

Local exhaust, fume capture, pressure relationships, make-up air and impacts on existing ventilation systems.

04

Specialty Gases & Vacuum

Gas type, pressure, flow, storage, distribution, point-of-use connections and laboratory vacuum requirements.

05

Water, Drainage & Process Cooling

Water quality, drainage, condensate, purified water, chilled water or dedicated process-cooling requirements.

06

Structural & Vibration

Equipment weight, floor loading, anchorage, vibration sensitivity and requirements for stable instrument performance.

07

Space, Access & Service Clearances

Delivery routes, doors, elevators, lifting, room dimensions, maintenance access and safe operating clearances.

08

BAS, Monitoring & Alarms

Environmental monitoring, equipment alarms, remote notifications, communications dependencies and after-hours response.

09

Commissioning & Turnover

Functional checks, utility verification, alarm testing, acceptance criteria and operational handover priorities.
