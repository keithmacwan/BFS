Flagship Service

Laboratory Infrastructure Risk Assessment

A structured engineering and scientific review of the infrastructure that protects laboratory operations, sensitive environments, critical equipment and high-value biological assets.

What happens when a system fails?

Traditional facility reviews often look at equipment in isolation. BFS evaluates how building infrastructure, laboratory operations and biological risk interact during credible failure scenarios.

The assessment can also become the starting point for engineering action. BFS can define technical scope, requirements, specifications and implementation steps needed to move a finding toward resolution.

When this service makes sense: recurring environmental issues, uncertainty about backup power or alarms, a new research program, major equipment procurement, or leadership needing a clearer infrastructure-risk and capital-priority picture.
