The BFS team

Co-Founder & Principal Engineer

Keith Macwan, M.Eng., P.Eng.

LinkedIn

Keith is a licensed Professional Engineer with more than a decade of experience in healthcare facilities, building systems, capital projects and operational readiness. At Trillium Health Partners, he supports mechanical, electrical, building automation, medical gas, pneumatic tube and other critical infrastructure across occupied hospital environments, with a focus on commissioning, turnover, shutdown planning, risk mitigation and long-term maintainability.

Previously, as a Control Systems Engineer with Johnson Controls, Keith delivered building automation and controls projects for major healthcare and institutional facilities from design development and submittal review through construction, commissioning, client training and handover. His earlier application-engineering experience strengthened his background in technical problem-solving, design validation and client-focused solutions.

Keith holds a Master of Engineering in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Toronto and a Bachelor of Engineering from Ryerson University. At BFS, he leads infrastructure risk assessments, resilience reviews and capital-planning studies that translate technical findings into practical priorities for laboratory and facility leaders.

Co-Founder | Laboratory Science

Vinitha Macwan, MSc

LinkedIn

Vinitha is a cell-biology researcher who brings the laboratory-user perspective to BFS. Her experience in research environments gives her a practical understanding of how facility conditions influence cell culture, biological samples, sensitive equipment, cold storage and day-to-day laboratory workflows.

At BFS, Vinitha helps connect scientific requirements to facility decisions by asking what the research actually needs to remain viable during normal operations and during failures. Her perspective helps assessments consider sample sensitivity, environmental excursions, storage and backup strategies, workflow, equipment dependencies and the operational consequences of losing temperature control, power, monitoring or other critical utilities.

By pairing laboratory science with engineering analysis, Vinitha helps ensure recommendations are not limited to whether building systems are technically functional, but also consider what infrastructure performance means for the science being protected.
