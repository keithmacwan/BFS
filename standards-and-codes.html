Standards and codes for critical facility infrastructure.

BioFacility Solutions uses the applicable code, standard, authority, client and manufacturer requirements to establish a project-specific technical basis for healthcare, laboratory, research and other critical environments.

Basis

Each engagement starts by confirming the applicable jurisdiction, adopted edition, authority having jurisdiction, client requirements and project-specific design criteria.

How BFS uses standards

Establish the right technical basis before making recommendations.

Standards are not applied as a generic checklist. BFS identifies which requirements are relevant to the facility, system, research activity and project scope, then documents that basis in the assessment, design criteria, specifications or commissioning plan.

Project-specific applicability

The newest published standard is not automatically the governing requirement.

Applicable codes and standards depend on jurisdiction, adopted editions, permits, authority-having-jurisdiction requirements, client standards, accreditation needs, equipment manufacturer requirements and the agreed engineering scope. BFS confirms and documents the appropriate design basis for each engagement.

Standards become useful when they are translated into a clear project basis.

BFS can help identify the applicable technical requirements, document gaps, develop engineering criteria and specifications, and verify implementation through review and commissioning support.
